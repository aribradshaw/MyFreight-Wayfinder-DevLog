  1. #v0.1.2 13 November 2026 Latest

    Rivals, treaties and a living sea

    • Two rival houses with real personalities now bid against you: Pemberton & Vane hold their rates like the old guard, while Keelcut Freight undercuts everyone and learns what you charge.
    • Sealed bids are fair: every offer is judged together when the window closes, and when you lose you learn who won and by how much.
    • Diplomacy arrives: agency agreements, co-loading, space sharing, referrals, non-compete zones, joint ventures, alliances with price wars, and hostile takeovers.
    • Pack shared containers in the new consolidation puzzle: weigh, stack and segregate cargo, then slam the doors for a SEALED stamp.
    • Play a five-chapter campaign, from your first charter through peak season, a canal blockage and agents abroad.
    • Multiplayer: host a world, share a room code, and run rival houses together with chat, pause requests and speed votes.
    • The sea comes alive: tides breathe along the coasts, the chart wraps endlessly across the Pacific, and storms, strikes and blockages appear on the map.
    • Real ship horns, a ship's telegraph bell, harbour ambience and music that tightens as cutoffs close in; no more overlapping text anywhere.
    Logged by Ari Bradshaw
  2. #v0.1.1 13 November 2026

    The Captain's Log opens: everything aboard so far

    • A living nautical world chart: ships sail real sea lanes between the great ports, and you can zoom from the whole globe down to a single harbour.
    • Run your forwarding desk: quote shippers, mind every document, cargo and VGM cutoff, and watch a missed sailing roll your boxes to the next vessel.
    • Rival forwarders bid against you in sealed offers, and you can strike referral pacts with them (or break them, at a price to your reputation).
    • A map-first desk: floating drawers, a folder card for every file, and text and numbers that roll, type and ease instead of snapping.
    • A main menu and a guided tutorial, plus sound: paper, stamps, telegraph keys and ship's bells.
    • Stakes and progression: working capital, covenant strikes, charter ranks, crew to hire and release, branch offices, and a verdict when your run ends.
    Logged by Ari Bradshaw d15990e
